**Export Selected to DXF v1.3.1**: sheet-metal fix.

- **Sheet-metal parts without a flat pattern now export their flat pattern.** Before, selecting a sheet-metal body whose component had no flat pattern yet exported only its largest flat face. The add-in now creates a temporary flat pattern (the largest flat face stays put), exports it with your bend-line settings, and removes it again, so the design is left as it was.
- Restart wording updated for Fusion's newer Scripts and Add-Ins dialog: turn the add-in's **Run** switch off, then on.

**Download:** ExportSelectedToDXF.zip below, or click **install update** in the panel if you're on v1.3.0.
