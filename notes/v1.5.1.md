**Export Selected to DXF v1.5.1**: panel polish.

- **About section.** The documentation, download latest and all versions links, a new link showing the installed version ("Export Selected to DXF (v1.5.1)", click for its release notes), the credit and **reset to defaults** are now together in a collapsible **About** section, closed by default. Its header shows the installed version. The links' tooltips now open upward.
- **Panel height fixed.** If Fusion refuses a resize (for example while the panel is still docking as it opens), the panel now retries until the height fits, instead of staying too tall.

**Download:** ExportSelectedToDXF.zip below, or click **install update** in the panel.
