**Export Selected to DXF v1.5.1**: panel polish.

- **About section.** The links and credit moved into a collapsible **About** section, closed by default, whose header shows the installed version. Inside: **documentation** and **Release Notes** (the notes for the version you have installed) on the first row, **download latest** and **all versions** on the second.
- **Reset in the corner.** **reset to defaults** now sits in the panel's top-right corner.
- **Panel height fixed.** The panel is no longer forced to dock when it opens (a docked panel's height is locked), it keeps retrying for up to a minute if Fusion refuses a resize, and the extra space below the last section is gone.

**Download:** ExportSelectedToDXF.zip below, or click **install update** in the panel.
