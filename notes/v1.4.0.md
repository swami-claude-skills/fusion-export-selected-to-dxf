Released: Nov 1, 2026

**Export Selected to DXF v1.4.0**: activity log, and flat patterns for sheet-metal parts that don't have one yet.

- **Log window.** A new **Log** section in the panel lists every export (each file written, skipped item and error), update checks and installs, with times. Show everything or problems only; **copy** puts the log on the clipboard to paste into a message; **open log file** opens the full history (log.txt in the add-in's settings folder, kept across restarts and updates).
- **Sheet-metal parts without a flat pattern now export their flat pattern.** Before, selecting such a body exported only its largest flat face. The add-in now creates the flat pattern (the largest flat face stays put) and leaves it in the part, exactly like Fusion's own Create Flat Pattern, then exports it with your bend-line settings.
- Restart wording updated for Fusion's newer Scripts and Add-Ins dialog: turn the add-in's **Run** switch off, then on.

**Download:** ExportSelectedToDXF.zip below, or click **install update** in the panel if you're on v1.3.0.
