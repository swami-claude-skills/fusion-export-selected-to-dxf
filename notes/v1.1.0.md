Released: Oct 30, 2026

**Export Selected DXF v1.1.0**: fixes from the first live test.

- **Selection count fixed.** The panel's selection readout stopped updating after the first change; it now follows every click.
- **Faces export cleanly.** Face outlines are no longer doubled.
- **Partial sketches fixed.** Exporting some curves of a sketch built on a face now works, keeping the curves at their original position.
- **Solid bodies.** Selecting a whole solid body exports its largest flat face.
