**Export Selected to DXF v1.5.0**: tidier panel.

- **Export section.** The selection summary, EXPORT SELECTED button and destination line are now a collapsible **Export** section (open by default); its header shows the selection at a glance, e.g. "4 selected → 4 files".
- **Panel fits its contents.** The panel's height follows the sections you open and close (when floating; a docked panel keeps the height its dock gives it).
- **Header links.** "dashboard" is now **documentation**, and all three links show a tooltip explaining where they go.
- **Log controls on top.** The filter, copy, open log file and clear controls sit above the log instead of below it, and the Log section always starts closed.
