**Export Selected to DXF v1.2.0**: first public release.

**Download:** ExportSelectedToDXF.zip below. Unzip, double-click `install-macos.command` (Mac) or `install-windows.cmd` (Windows), then in Fusion press Shift+S, choose ExportSelectedToDXF and click Run.

**What's in it**
- One DXF per selected item: whole sketches, some curves of a sketch, planar faces, solid bodies (largest flat face) and sheet-metal flat patterns
- Units (design, mm, cm, m, in, ft), file naming, overwrite or auto-number, construction/points/projected geometry options
- Button in SOLID ▸ CREATE and UTILITIES ▸ ADD-INS
- Panel links to the [dashboard](https://swami-claude-skills.github.io/fusion-export-selected-to-dxf/) and the latest download, and shows a banner when a newer version is released

**Upgrading from ExportSelectedDXF:** the installer retires the old add-in and your settings carry over; set your keyboard shortcut again.
